Claire’s story: One year on from a breast cancer diagnosis, Claire has turned something pretty rubbish into something positive.

Hi, I’m Claire. A year ago, my world was turned upside down when I was diagnosed with breast cancer. I’ve now finished active treatment, though my journey continues with targeted and hormone therapy.

I’ve shared it all over on @clairevsboobiecancer. Somewhere along the way, I decided I wanted to give something back and so these little caps were born.

Two cheeky caps.
One big F**k you
to cancer.

Breast cancer is Claire’s personal story, but cancer affects so many people in so many ways. The F**k Cancer cap is for anyone going through it, supporting someone they love, remembering someone special, or simply wanting to give cancer the middle finger.

Questions

Where does the money go?

All proceeds from every cap go to Breast Cancer Now, the UK charity that funds breast cancer research and supports people affected by the disease.

How much are the caps?

Each cap is £10. Postage is added at checkout, and click & collect is free.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Not at the moment. Delivery is UK only.

How does click & collect work?

Choose click & collect on the product page and pay at checkout. Claire will email you to arrange collection.

What size are the caps?

One size. Each pink baseball cap has an adjustable strap.

What if I want to return my cap?

Get in touch and Claire will sort it out.
